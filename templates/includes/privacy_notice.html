Data Privacy Notice

Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

Welcome, {{ user.get_display_name }}. The {{ agency.system_name }} of the {{ agency.office }} holds personal information about local government officials, focal persons and DILG personnel. Before you continue, please read the terms under which you may use it.

1. What the system collects

Your name, username, e-mail address, role and office; the records you create, edit, upload or approve; and a log of your sign-ins and actions, including the date, time and network address used. This is processed only to monitor and evaluate local government programmes and to keep the system secure and accountable.

2. Your obligations as a user

  • Access and use personal information only as your official duties require.
  • Do not copy, download, print, share or disclose records to anyone not authorised to receive them.
  • Keep your password and authenticator codes to yourself, and sign out when you leave your computer.
  • Keep any exported or printed records secure, and dispose of them properly when no longer needed.
  • Report any suspected data breach, loss or unauthorised access to the system administrator at once.

3. Rights of data subjects

Under Section 16 of the Act, the people whose information is kept here have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to correct, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to be indemnified for damages. Treat every record accordingly.

4. Monitoring and penalties

All activity in this system is recorded in the audit log. Unauthorised processing, access or disclosure of personal information, and negligence that leads to it, are punishable by imprisonment and fines under Chapter VIII of Republic Act No. 10173, in addition to administrative sanctions.

5. Questions

For questions about this notice or how personal information is handled, contact the {{ agency.office_short }} at {{ agency.email }}.
