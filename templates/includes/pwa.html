Install {{ agency.system_name }}

  1. Tap the browser menu ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.
  3. Open the system from the new icon on your home screen.
  1. Click the browser menu ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Choose Cast, save and share → Create shortcut…
  3. Tick Open as window, then click Create.
  1. Click the browser menu … at the top right.
  2. Choose More tools → Pin to taskbar (or Pin to Start).
  3. Open the system from the new icon on your taskbar.

Use this browser’s menu to add the page to your home screen or desktop, or open it in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge for an app window.

This address is not secure (HTTP), so the browser offers a shortcut rather than a full install. Ask the system administrator for the HTTPS address for the full app.

Install {{ agency.system_name }}

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari’s toolbar (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The system then opens from its own icon, full screen.

On iPhone and iPad this works in Safari only.
