{% load ui %} {% comment %} --------------------------------------------------------------------------- One activity as it appears inside a day cell. A link to the record, first and always - scripting upgrades it to a preview dialog by reading the data- attributes below, and if scripting is unavailable the click still opens the activity. Nothing here is rendered that the viewer is not permitted to see; the queryset settled that. The title wraps rather than truncating. "1:30PM SGL..." tells a reader nothing they came to the calendar for; three lines of a real title tells them what the day holds without opening anything. The row grows to fit, which is what a calendar with real entries in it should do. Three lines is the ceiling, not the target: titles run to 255 characters in the model, and one pathological entry should not push a week off the screen. Anything longer is still in the tooltip, the dialog and the record. The colour band names the activity type and the left edge marks priority. Neither carries meaning on its own: the title is always the label, and the preview spells out type, owner and status in words. `relative z-10` lifts the chip above the day cell's overlay link, so a click on an activity opens that activity while a click anywhere else in the box opens the day. In the week strip, where there is no overlay, it does nothing. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- {% endcomment %}
