Message from the Division Chief

RAY GREGORY F. JARANILLA

Chief, Local Government Monitoring and Evaluation Division
DILG Region XIII - Caraga

Welcome to the official microsite of the Local Government Monitoring and Evaluation Division (LGMED) of DILG Region XIII. As the monitoring arm of DILG Caraga, our mission is to ensure that local governance is not just a process, but a tangible service that reaches every citizen. We believe that what gets measured gets improved; therefore, we provide the evidence-based feedback and oversight necessary to help our Local Government Units thrive and improve their service delivery.

This platform is designed to provide our partners—the Local Government Units, Civil Society Organizations, and the general public—with real-time access to vital reports, operational guidelines, and regional milestones. Together, let us continue to uphold the highest standards of transparency, accountability, and excellence in the Caraga Region.
