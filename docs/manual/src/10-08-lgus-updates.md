## 10.8 LGU Management

### A. Module overview

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Where | **Sidebar › LGU Management** (`/app/lgus/`) |
| Purpose | The directory of the LGUs of Region XIII (5 provinces, 6 cities and 67 municipalities): type, province, income class, contact person, compliance status and monitoring history. |
| Who | All roles can view. Encoder, LGMED Staff and Administrators can add and edit. Administrators can delete. |
| Compliance statuses | Compliant, Partially compliant, Non-compliant, Not yet assessed. |

### B. Visual interface guide

{{figure:lgus-list}}

{{figure:lgus-detail}}

### C. Procedures

### Procedure 10.8.1: View an LGU's profile and monitoring history

**Who can do this:** All roles.

**Steps:**

1. Click **LGU Management** in the sidebar.
2. Search by name or contact person, or filter by **Province**, **LGU type** or **Compliance**, then click **Apply**.
3. Click the LGU's name (or the eye icon).

**Expected Result:** The LGU's **LGU Profile**, **Monitoring History**, **Contact Information** and **Record Information** are shown. **New record** starts a monitoring record.

### Procedure 10.8.2: Add or edit an LGU

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Add LGU** (or **Edit** on an LGU).
2. Under **LGU Identification**, enter **LGU name\***, **LGU type\***, **Province\***, **Income class** and tick **Independent of the province** for an independent city.
3. Under **Contact Information**, enter the address, contact person, position, number and e-mail.
4. Under **Oversight**, choose the **Compliance status\***, keep **Active in the directory** ticked, and add **Notes**.
5. Click **Save**.

**Expected Result:** The LGU is saved and listed in the directory and in LGU drop-down lists throughout the system.

**Visual Reference:** Figure {{ref:lgus-new}}.

**Important Notes:** To take an LGU out of use without losing its history, untick **Active in the directory** rather than deleting it. The LGU roster loaded with the system must be checked against the office's official records.

## 10.9 Frontline Services

### A. Module overview

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Where | **Sidebar › Frontline Services** (`/app/services/`) |
| Purpose | The Division's frontline services — who may avail, requirements, processing time, fees and the responsible office — for listing on the public website. |
| Who | All roles can view. Encoder, LGMED Staff and Administrators can add and edit. Administrators can delete. |

### B. Visual interface guide

{{figure:services-list}}

### C. Procedures

### Procedure 10.9.1: Add or edit a frontline service

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Frontline Services › Add Service** (or **Edit** on a service).
2. Under **Service Information**, enter **Service name\***, **Service type\*** (Simple transaction, Complex transaction, Highly technical) and **Description**.
3. Under **Requirements and Processing**, enter **Clients**, **Requirements**, **Processing time** and **Fees**.
4. Under **Responsibility**, enter the **Responsible office** and **Responsible person**.
5. Tick **Published on the public website** to list it publicly.
6. Click **Save**.

**Expected Result:** The service is saved. If published, it appears on the public website's **Frontline Services** page.

## 10.10 Updates & Accomplishments

### A. Module overview

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Where | **Sidebar › Accomplishments** (`/app/updates/`): the division dashboard; **Division Updates** (entries); **Reporting Weeks**; the Chief's **Review**; the **Convocation view**; **Public disclosure** |
| Purpose | The Division's official weekly record of its own work. Staff contribute activities, communications, accomplishments, photographs and other evidence, POPS Plan progress, ways forward and upcoming activities to **one weekly record for the whole Division**. The record is updated every **Thursday** and presented at the **Monday convocation**. |
| Who | **Contribute** (add entries, evidence, POPS Plan rows, ways forward; submit the week): Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators. **Review, select for the convocation, clear for the public, publish, set the disclosure window**: LGMED Staff and Administrators. Viewers read only. |
| Week statuses | Open for contributions; For the Chief's review; Reviewed; Published. |
| Entry statuses | Completed, Ongoing, Pending, Upcoming. |

> **IMPORTANT:** This is a **division accomplishment record, not an employee performance monitor**. The figures belong to the Division; no figure is calculated per staff member. The *Focal person* on an entry only says who can answer for it.

### B. Visual interface guide

{{figure:updates-dashboard}}

{{figure:updates-week}}

### C. Procedures

### Procedure 10.10.1: Contribute an entry to the week

**Purpose:** Record something the Division did, received, sent or is about to do.

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Accomplishments › Add Update**.
2. Under **Reporting Period**, choose the **Reporting period\*** (the week).
3. Under **What the Division Did**, enter the **Title\***, **Category\***, **Activity type\*** and **Status\***.
4. Under **When and Where**, enter the **Date\*** (and **End date**), **Location** and **LGU concerned**.
5. Under **Details**, describe **What was accomplished** and any **Remarks**.
6. Under **People**, choose the **Focal person** and list **Assigned personnel / contributors** and any **Partner office or agency**.
7. Under **Communications and References**, enter the **Reference number** and **From / to** for communications.
8. Click **Save**.

**Expected Result:** The entry is added to the week's **Consolidated Division Updates**.

**Visual Reference:** Figure {{ref:updates-new}}.

**Important Notes:** The date must fall inside the chosen week. Only an **Upcoming** entry may be dated after it; upcoming work is not counted as an accomplishment.

{{figure:updates-new}}

### Procedure 10.10.2: File a means of verification (evidence)

**Purpose:** Attach the photograph, issuance, report, certificate, attendance sheet or letter that proves an accomplishment.

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Open the entry (or, on the week, click **File evidence for …** beside it in **Awaiting a Means of Verification**).
2. Under **Add a Means of Verification**, choose the **Type of evidence\***, choose the **File\*** and describe **What it shows**.
3. Tick **Cleared for the public website** only if the file may be shown publicly.
4. Click **File the evidence**.

**Expected Result:** The file is listed under **Means of Verification**; photographs appear in the week's photo gallery.

**Important Notes:** Evidence is not required to save an entry, but entries without it are listed under **Awaiting a Means of Verification** and counted in the Division's statistics. A file filed as a *Photograph* must be an image.

{{figure:updates-entry}}

### Procedure 10.10.3: Add POPS Plan progress and ways forward

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Open the week (**Accomplishments › Reporting weeks**, then the week).
2. In **POPS Plan Updates**, click **Add**. Enter the **Commitment** (the POPS Plan commitment or indicator), the **Target** and the number **Accomplished** (whole numbers), any **Remarks**, and whether it may be shown publicly. Save.
3. In **Ways Forward**, click **Add**. Under **Next Step**, enter the description and detail; under **Tracking**, the target date, status (Pending, Ongoing, Completed) and whether it may be shown publicly. Save.

**Important Notes:** The POPS Plan compliance percentage is calculated from the targets and accomplishments; it is never typed by hand.

**Expected Result:** The rows appear on the week and in the convocation view.

### Procedure 10.10.4: Submit the week for the Chief's review

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Open the week. Check **The Division's Week** panel, which lists which of the seven parts of the week are recorded and which are still missing.
2. Click **Submit for review**.

**Expected Result:** The week's status becomes **For the Chief's review**.

### Procedure 10.10.5: Review the week and select what is presented

**Purpose:** The Division Chief's review: theme, remarks, convocation selection and public clearance.

**Who can do this:** LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Open the week and click **Review**.
2. Under **Remarks & Theme**, enter the **Theme** and **Division Chief's remarks**, then click **Save remarks**.
3. Under **Major Accomplishments to Present**, tick the accomplishments to present at the Monday convocation and click **Save selection**. The page warns you if a selected accomplishment has no means of verification.
4. Under **Public Clearance**, tick the entries and photographs the public may see and click **Save clearance**.
5. To publish, click **Publish to the public website**. To send the week back to contributors, click **Reopen for contributions**.

**Expected Result:** The convocation view leads with the selected accomplishments. A published week appears on the public website's **Accomplishments** page, showing only the items that were cleared.

**Visual Reference:** Figure {{ref:updates-review}}.

**Important Notes:** Selecting for the convocation and clearing for the public are **separate decisions**. A week cannot be published with nothing cleared. Reopening a published week withdraws it from the public website. Staff names and the Chief's remarks are never published.

{{figure:updates-review}}

### Procedure 10.10.6: Present the week at the Monday convocation

**Who can do this:** All roles can open the view; changing the selection requires review rights.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Accomplishments › Convocation view** (or **Convocation view** on a week).
2. Present the page on a screen; it is set in large type and has no editing controls.
3. Click **Print** for a paper copy, or **Change the selection** to return to the review page.

{{figure:updates-convocation}}

### Procedure 10.10.7: Set the public disclosure window

**Purpose:** Decide how far back the public website's Accomplishments page goes.

**Who can do this:** LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Accomplishments › Public disclosure**.
2. Choose **What the public website shows\***: *The most recent completed weeks*, *The most recent completed months*, *A fixed range of dates*, or *Every week the Division has published*.
3. Enter the **Number of weeks**, **Number of months**, or **Showing from** / **Showing until**, as applicable.
4. Tick **Also show the week that is still running** only if the current week should be public.
5. Check **Showing Now** and click **Save**.

**Expected Result:** The public website shows exactly the weeks listed under **Showing Now**.

**Important Notes:** Only published weeks can ever be shown. A fixed range with a missing date shows nothing rather than everything.

{{figure:updates-disclosure}}

## 10.11 Announcements

### A. Module overview

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Where | **Sidebar › Announcements** (`/app/announcements/`) |
| Purpose | News, advisories, issuances, activity reports and commendations for the public website's **News** page, which also leads the homepage. |
| Who | All roles can view. Encoder, LGMED Staff and Administrators can add and edit. Administrators can delete. |

### B. Visual interface guide

{{figure:announcements-list}}

### C. Procedures

### Procedure 10.11.1: Write and publish an announcement

**Who can do this:** Encoder, LGMED Staff, Administrators.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Announcements › Add Announcement**.
2. Under **Item Details**, enter the **Headline\***, **Category\*** (News, Advisory, Issuance, Activity, Commendation), **Date published\*** and **Location**.
3. Under **Text**, write the **Lead paragraph\*** and the **Full text**.
4. Under **Photograph and Source**, choose a **Photograph**, describe it, and enter **Read more at** if there is a source link.
5. Under **Publication**, tick **Published on the public website** (untick to keep a draft) and, if wanted, **Feature on the homepage**.
6. Click **Save**.

**Expected Result:** A published item appears on the public **News** page from its publication date. The record page shows its public address.

**Visual Reference:** Figure {{ref:announcements-new}}.

**Important Notes:** A draft, or an item dated in the future, is not visible to the public by any route. Photographs: JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF.

{{figure:announcements-new}}
